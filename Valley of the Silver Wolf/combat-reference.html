Confirmed So Far

  • Combat takes place in a dedicated modal above the map and roster page.
  • A clash has five phases: Selection, Reveal, Reaction, Calculation, Activation.
  • Digital “place a stone” effects will become Form Point deductions.
  • Both fighters must always be able to see their remaining Hit Points and Form Points.
  • Fight is only available when the active player shares a space with at least one other living player.
  • If multiple opponents share the space, the challenger chooses who to challenge.
  • The challenged player may accept or decline the challenge.
  • Declining a challenge lowers Reputation by 1.
